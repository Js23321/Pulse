What's inside

Everything you need. Nothing to distract you.

Six modules, all built around the same idea: do a little, every single day.

Habit streaks
Check off daily habits and watch your streak grow. Consistency beats intensity every time — this is just the proof.
Measurable goals
Pick a target, log your progress, see the line move. No vague aspirations — just numbers you can actually trust.
Focus timers
Pomodoro blocks, study sessions, workout intervals. Structured time so you stop faffing around and actually start.

An AI coach that's already read your data before you ask. Not generic advice — your numbers, reflected back at you.
Activity notes
Write notes during sessions, attach them to goals, or keep a running journal. Your context stays with your work.

Why it works

Motivation fades. Showing up doesn't.
